## Quickstart

1. Download the file for your system and run it.
2. The ship sits at the center with two blue rings around it. These are the inner and
   outer edges of the warp bubble. Particles enter from the right.
3. Drag the **Radius** slider in the **Bubble** tab and watch the trajectories change.
4. Press <kbd>Space</kbd> to pause. Scroll to zoom, hold the right mouse button to pan,
   and click the middle button to reset the view.
5. Click a particle to tag it. It turns magenta and stays visible.
6. Open the **Save & Load** tab and click **Dump Single Checkpoint**. You now have a
   `dump.bin` holding the exact state of every particle.

You should see red-to-yellow particles curving around a blue ring, with the ship holding
station at the center. If the window is black, or the program reports that it found no
graphics adapter, read [Install and run](manual/install.html).

The [ten-minute tutorial](manual/first-simulation.html) covers the same ground more
carefully and ends with data you can plot.
