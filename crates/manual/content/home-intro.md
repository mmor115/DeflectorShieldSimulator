The Alcubierre warp drive is usually discussed as a way to travel faster than light. This
simulator asks a different question, and a more practical one: at sub-light speed, does
the warp bubble deflect the debris a ship would otherwise run into?

The program integrates the geodesics of massive particles and photons through a
warp-drive metric modified to act as a deflector shield. You change the shield geometry
with sliders and watch the trajectories bend in real time. When a trajectory interests
you, tag it, export it, and re-run it deterministically from any saved checkpoint.

It accompanies the paper below, and the physics comes from the same
[`deflector-core`](https://github.com/lucass-carneiro/DeflectorShields) crate the paper
used.
