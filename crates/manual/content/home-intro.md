The Alcubierre warp drive is usually discussed as a way to travel faster than light. This
simulator asks a different question, and a more practical one: at sub-light speed, does
the warp bubble protect the ship from interstellar debris?

The program integrates the geodesics of massive particles and photons through a
warp-drive metric with an optional deflector-shield component, constructed in the
same style as the drive itself. You change the parameters of the drive and shield
with sliders and watch the trajectories bend in real time.
When a trajectory interests you, tag it, export it, and re-run it deterministically
from any saved checkpoint.

The physics comes from the same
[`deflector-core`](https://github.com/lucass-carneiro/DeflectorShields) crate as the paper
below.
